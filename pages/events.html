Events

Annual Classic Car Show

Join us for our Annual Classic Car Show, a celebration of automotive history and design. This event showcases a stunning array of vintage and classic cars from various eras, meticulously restored and maintained by passionate collectors. Attendees will have the opportunity to admire these timeless vehicles, learn about their histories, and engage with fellow car enthusiasts. The event also features live music, food stalls, and family-friendly activities, making it a perfect day out for car lovers of all ages. Whether you're a seasoned collector or simply appreciate the beauty of classic automobiles, the Annual Classic Car Show promises an unforgettable experience.



Special Events

Throughout the year, Wheels Of Fire hosts a variety of special events designed to engage and excite our community of car enthusiasts. From exclusive track days where members can experience high-performance driving under expert supervision, to themed meet-ups that celebrate specific marques or automotive eras, there's always something happening at our venue. We also organize educational workshops and seminars led by industry professionals, covering topics such as car restoration, maintenance, and the latest automotive technologies. Keep an eye on our events calendar to stay updated on upcoming activities and make sure you don't miss out on these unique opportunities to connect with fellow car lovers and deepen your automotive knowledge.

Vintage Rally

Experience the thrill of the Vintage Rally, where classic cars take to the open road in a scenic journey through picturesque landscapes. This event is designed for vintage car enthusiasts who want to showcase their prized vehicles while enjoying a leisurely drive. Participants will follow a predetermined route, stopping at various checkpoints that highlight local attractions and historical sites. The rally emphasizes camaraderie among drivers and offers opportunities for socializing, photography, and celebrating the heritage of vintage automobiles. Whether you're driving or spectating, the Vintage Rally is a unique way to connect with automotive history and fellow enthusiasts.

Supercar Weekend

Get ready for an adrenaline-packed Supercar Weekend, where the most exotic and high-performance vehicles come together for an unforgettable experience. This event features a dazzling lineup of supercars from renowned manufacturers, allowing attendees to see, hear, and even ride in some of the fastest cars on the planet. Highlights of the weekend include dynamic driving experiences, track days, and exclusive showcases where owners share insights about their vehicles. Car enthusiasts will also enjoy meet-and-greet sessions with industry experts, photo opportunities, and merchandise stalls. Supercar Weekend is the ultimate celebration of speed, luxury, and automotive excellence.
